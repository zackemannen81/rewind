
# RE:WIND Asset Requirements

This document lists the required art and sound assets for the project, based on the GDD.

## Art Assets

### Characters
- Player Character (androgynous, minimalist silhouette)
- Guard (functional, geometric design)
- Drone (flying, geometric design)

### Environment (Chapter 1)
- Apartment 4C props (bed, table, radio, etc.)
- Hallway and staircase elements
- Courtyard props (generator, benches, foliage)
- Street props (kiosk, signs, streetlights)
- Transit Hub props (turnstiles, screens)

### UI (Diegetic)
- Wristwatch model
- Terminal models (desktop, wall-mounted)
- Anslagstavla (notice board) model

### Effects
- Muzzle flashes (for guards, if any)
- Impact effects
- Time loop visual effects (glitching, wireframe, desaturation)
- Echo/ghost material and shader

## Sound Assets

### Music
- Ambient, atmospheric tracks for exploration
- Tense, pulsing tracks for stealth and end-of-loop sequences

### Sound Effects
- Player footfalls (walking, running, sneaking on different surfaces)
- Player interactions (button presses, lever pulls, etc.)
- Time loop events (start, end, reset, paradox glitch)
- Enemy sounds (drone whirring, guard footsteps, alert sounds)
- Environmental sounds (rain, wind, machinery hum)

### Voice & Radio
- Processed radio messages (for codes and narrative)
- Ljudloggar (audio logs) with a cassette/damaged file effect
